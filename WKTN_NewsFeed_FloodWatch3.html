Storm Approaches: Riverside Relief Prepares for Flooding
VIDEO

Forecasters say a severe storm will bring major flooding to the Cumberland River region in the coming days, and local officials are issuing a flood watch. At Riverside Relief, staff members are preparing for approximately 1,200 people who may need assistance. Based on anticipated needs, the organization estimates that bottled water, baby formula, first-aid supplies, and hygiene kits will be among the most needed items.
