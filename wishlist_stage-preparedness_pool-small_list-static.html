Help Riverside Relief Prepare for Cumberland River Flooding

As seen on WKTN News 5 — Riverside Relief's full supply list, updated live.

Riverside Relief

Regional Nonprofit

Riverside Relief is a regional nonprofit helping communities along the Cumberland River prepare for and recover from flooding. It has posted this list of supplies so people who want to help can choose specific items to have shipped directly to where they're needed.

Riverside Relief posted this list on a local community board. About 30 people have viewed it so far. Roughly 20% of what the shelter needs has been donated already.
